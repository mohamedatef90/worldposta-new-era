CloudSpace Datasheet

A smarter, more connected way to work.

Product overview

CloudSpace brings files, communication, calendars, notes, documents and tasks together in one secure workspace.

  • Drive: Store, share and collaborate on documents, spreadsheets, presentations and PDFs.
  • Connect: Team chat, video meetings, channels, audio calls and screen sharing.
  • Calendar: Shared schedules and meeting planning.
  • Notes: Meeting notes, project ideas, action items and personal notes.
  • Documents: Create and edit together in real time.
  • Tasks: Assign work and track progress.

Use cases

Keep distributed teams connected, organize project files, coordinate meetings and collaborate on business documents.

Security and administration
